Send a deletion request

Email brentjacob.malolot@sccpag.edu.ph using the subject CampusAlert account deletion request. State that you want your CampusAlert account and all associated information deleted. Use the institutional account you registered with when possible. If you cannot access it, explain that without sending sensitive documents.

Open deletion request email

The link opens your email application. It does not send the message or delete your account automatically. You must send the email yourself. Do not include your password, one-time codes, school ID number, report contents, or photos.

Who processes it and when

Brent receives your request and forwards it to the school office for validation and processing. Deletion is completed within 3–5 weeks from the date the request is submitted, not from office approval. Keep a copy of your sent request.

What is deleted

All information associated with your CampusAlert account is deleted, including account details and associated reports and information. None of that associated information is retained after deletion is completed.

Deletion applies to your CampusAlert information, not your institutional Google account. Reports are otherwise archived indefinitely for maintenance records; this does not create an exception to account/data deletion.
