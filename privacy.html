Overview

CampusAlert is a CIIL student project for campus infrastructure and facility-maintenance reporting at Saint Columban College. This policy explains how information is handled when students, assigned maintenance staff and administrators use the app.

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Last updated: October 21, 2026

Information we store

CampusAlert uses Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In with institutional @sccpag.edu.ph accounts. It stores your name, institutional email, student ID number and account role in Firebase. It does not store your home address.

Report information includes problem descriptions, attached photos, the selected campus building or area, and staff resolution notes.

Why information is used

Information supports account access, facility reporting, repair-task assignment, resolution notes, status updates, and maintenance analytics. Firebase Cloud Messaging provides push notifications about report status.

Campus area, not GPS

CampusAlert does not use GPS or device-location tracking. You select the affected campus building or area from a dropdown, such as Library, Gym or Canteen, and describe the issue. This selected facility information is part of the report, not a tracked device position.

On-device priority classification

The local PriorityClassifier uses TensorFlow Lite on your smartphone to read a report description and estimate High, Medium or Low priority. The description is not sent to an external AI provider for this classification. Report descriptions and other submitted information are still stored and synchronized through Cloud Firestore for the maintenance workflow.

Who can see information

Students can view only reports they submitted. Staff can view only repair tasks assigned to them. Administrators can oversee all campus reports, assign staff and view maintenance analytics.

Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging provide account access, cloud data synchronization and notifications. Google/Firebase services process information needed to perform these functions.

Retention and deletion

Resolved and unresolved reports are archived indefinitely for campus maintenance and facility records unless an account/data deletion request is submitted. Completed and Pending are workflow statuses, not deletion schedules.

All information associated with a requested CampusAlert account, including account details and associated report information, is deleted within 3–5 weeks from the date the request is submitted. No associated information is retained after deletion is completed. This deletion period starts at submission, not school-office approval.

Request account deletion

Email brentjacob.malolot@sccpag.edu.ph with the subject CampusAlert account deletion request. Brent receives the request and forwards it to the school office for validation and processing.

Read the account-deletion request instructions. Logging out is not account deletion.

Security and choices

CampusAlert uses Google/Firebase authentication, role-based access and automatic logout after approximately 2.5 minutes of inactivity to help protect accounts. Logout ends a session but does not delete the account or reports. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact the project for privacy questions, access or correction requests, or questions about student eligibility and notices for minors. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, school IDs or report contents in an email.
